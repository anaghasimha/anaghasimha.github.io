Some places I've been and a little about what made them interesting.

---

![Lower Antelope Canyon](/assets/img/IMG_4212.jpg)
*Lower Antelope Canyon, Page, AZ — A slot canyon carved by floodwaters over centuries, sacred to the Navajo people. The light filtering through the narrow openings and the water-sculpted walls are unlike anything I've seen.*

---

![Ganga Aarti, Varanasi](/assets/img/IMG_3577.jpg)
*Ganga Aarti, Varanasi — Thousands gathered on the ghats for the evening aarti, a synchronized ritual offering to the Ganges. The scale and energy of it was extraordinary.*

---

![Cold Spring, NY](/assets/img/IMG_3382.jpg)
*Cold Spring, NY — A peaceful fall hike with the Hudson Valley turning every shade of orange and red. The town is full of small, cozy shops, exactly the kind of place you stumble upon and want to stay.*
