I do not use GenAI to write/draft any of my posts . I believe that AI smoothes my voice and does not highlight my way of thinking and my ideas clearly. That said, I am not against the use of AI. I sometimes use AI to check for grammar mistakes, finding synonyms or even help me phrase sentences better. But all of the thoughts, ideas, narration are entirely mine. I have noted this in a couple of my posts, but I have forgotten to mention them in some of the other posts. So I figured I'd build an entire page dedicated to the use of AI in my work. This page will be updated if there are any changes to the policy.
