Toku was one among the earliest homo-sapiens who had discovered the power of language. He used a full set of words to describe different things to his community. His community, called Arks, were a group of 100 people. They discovered new words mostly based on the sounds made by nature. This new discovery of language to communicate different things was being used by them mainly to protect themselves in the forest and to capture prey.

One day, Toku stumbled upon what looked like a hat, but made of steel. Without him knowing what it was, he placed it on his head. A weird feeling washed over him. He was not in the forest anymore, in fact, he could hardly see any trees. He was completely surrounded by what looked like gigantic boxes. He could see his own reflection off of the buildings. He was surrounded with hundreds of people around him, all chattering continuously. These people looked like him, but were so different from him at the same time. Little did he know the hat-like object was a time machine and he was now in the 21st century.

Toku moved around the city. He saw people clad in weird material. He saw things being written on these so-called boxes. He was petrified of this place, but also curious. People approached him and talked to him. Poor Toku never used words like these people, he didn't understand even a word spoken. He was also confused why these humans spoke so much. Back in his place, people spoke only when necessary. Talking continuously was only a luxury for him as he had to bring home food and making too much noise in the forests would only bring him to danger. He tried talking in his own language but people around him didn't understand it either. Toku thought there was no way out of this gridlock. People around him were also confused, unable to help Toku. But lucky for him, the time machine malfunctioned and he was sent right back to his community in the forest. For a minute, he thought he was dreaming. His community was completely unaware of his absence.

Once back home, Toku gathered his community and told them his bizarre story. He only knew so many words, which was why he could hardly communicate whatever he experienced. He tried doing some actions and people around him thought Toku was going crazy. They thought he was asleep for too long which is why he was behaving so.

But how can a human even say something they have experienced when there were no words to describe them?
