Sherry was invited to a party by her close friend she knew from childhood, Nicole. They had grown together in the same town and went to the same university. Nicole loved having friends over, but this time around the party was huge. Sherry guessed at least two hundred. She arrived at 7 P.M. sharp, greeted Nicole and her husband and made herself comfortable at a table with other friends of the host. There was a lot of smiling, laughing and introducing one another.

Everyone at the table was involved in a deep discussion on makeup trends when Sarah pulled Erica to a corner. Sherry was watching the two and Erica sounded surprised -- she gasped and kept sighing. "Wonder what that was," thought Sherry. Anyhow, she was never one to gossip so she never bothered asking anyone.

A couple of minutes later, Sherry saw Erica whispering something to Carol. Carol was also shocked and flabbergasted. This went on and on until at least ten other people were involved in this little whispered secret. Miranda was the last to receive it and, much to everyone's surprise, she went ahead and whispered it back into Sarah's ears.

The reaction, however, was much different this time. Sarah was angry and was yelling at Erica and Miranda. The whole party saw the fight play out. Apparently, Sarah had said that Grace had lost her baby while she was pregnant. But Miranda received the message as Grace falling down the stairs and killing the baby. Grace, who was also at the party, started sobbing. Poor Grace -- there was no fault of hers. Sarah was embarrassed, but she had no way to figure out who had twisted her message.

Alas, there is no way to control gossip.
